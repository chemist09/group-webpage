Torsten John presented talks at three international conferences this summer: PepMat 2026 in Warsaw, the 10th EuChemS Chemistry Congress (ECC10) in Antwerp, and the Peptide Self-Assembly Conference 2026 in Mainz, co-hosted by the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research and Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz. PhD student Aiswarya Rajeev also attended the Mainz meeting.
